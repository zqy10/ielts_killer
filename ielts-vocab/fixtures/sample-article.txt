World leaders gathered in Geneva on Thursday as a climate summit opened amid mounting pressure to reach a deal. Delegates said financing remained the most contentious issue on the agenda, with several states yet to ratify the amended treaty. Analysts warned that without a coherent framework, disparities in funding would widen further. Officials pledged to mitigate the worst effects of warming, but critics argued that superficial reforms could not substantiate long-term resilience. The talks are expected to set a precedent for how emerging economies participate in future negotiations.
